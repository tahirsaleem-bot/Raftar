0Interview se pehle — 5 minute

  • Baithne ka andaaz: mez ke aar paar aamne saamne mat baithiye — thora side par. Aamne saamne baithna interview lagta hai, saath baithna baat cheet.
  • Phone silent aur darwaza band. Beech mein koi andar na aaye.
  • Notebook khol lijiye, magar pehle 5 minute likhiye mat. Jab woh dekhegi ke aap foran likhna shuru nahi kar rahe, guard giregi. Likhna tab shuru kijiye jab woh khud detail mein aa jaye.
  • Waqt: 35–45 minute rakhiye. Jaldi mat kijiye — asal baat aakhir ke 15 minute mein aati hai.
Sab se aham hidayat

Naveera ke interview mein jo baatein aayin, un ka zikr bilkul mat kijiye — na naam, na koi jumla, na ishara. Kuch sawaal un flags ko cross-check karte hain, magar woh sab khule andaaz mein rakhe gaye hain. Agar baat sach hai, woh khud saamne aa jayegi. Agar aap ne pehle bata diya, to aap ko sirf woh jawab milega jo woh dena chahti hai.

1Shuruaat — ye lafz parh dijiye

Opening — taqreeban isi tarah kahiye

"Sara, sab se pehle to shukriya ke aap ne itna waqt humare saath guzara.

Ye feedback session hai — ye har us bande ke saath hota hai jo ja raha hai, koi khaas baat nahi. Maqsad sirf ye hai ke aap ne jo dekha, us se idara kuch seekh sake.

Aap ki koi baat aap ke against use nahi hogi, na hi ye clearance ya reference se juri hai. Jo cheez aap flag karna chahengi, woh main likh kar aage bhejunga.

Aur aik baat — aap RM rahi hain, to aap ko woh cheezein nazar aayi hongi jo coaches ko nahi aatin. Wahi meri asal dilchaspi hai. Jitna khul kar bologi, utna faida hoga."

Phir 2–3 minute halki baat: "To aagay ka kya plan hai?" — seedha "kyun ja rahi ho" se shuru mat kijiye. Bandah khul jata hai.

2Janay ki wajah

1Ja kahan rahi hain, aur asal wajah kya bani?
Pehla jawab aksar bana hua hota hai. Sun lijiye, tokiye mat, phir agla sawaal.
2Us ke ilawa aur kuch? Aksar aik hi wajah nahi hoti.
Yahan se asal wajah nikalti hai. Ye sawaal poochna mat bhooliye — zyadatar log pehli wajah bata kar ruk jate hain.
3Aisi kya cheez hoti ke aap na jaatin?
Poore interview ka sab se qeemti sawaal. "Kyun ja rahi ho" ka jawab diplomatic hota hai — is ka sach hota hai.
Agar jawab ghol mol ho: "Faisla karne se pehle kisi se baat ki thi? Kya jawab mila?"

3RM hone ke naatay — jo sirf woh bata sakti hai

4Aap ki team ke jo coaches gaye ya ja rahe hain — aap ki nazar mein asal wajah kya hai?
Ye sawaal sirf RM se pooch sakte hain. Us ne apni team ke exits qareeb se dekhe hain, aur ab khud ja rahi hai — sach bolne mein ab kuch nahi rakha.
Follow-up: "Kya koi aisa pattern hai jo aap ko nazar aaya — ke log aik hi wajah se ja rahe hain?"
5Leadership se jo faisle aate thay, woh aap team tak pohanchati thin. Jab aap khud convinced na hotin — tab kya karti thin?
Is poore interview ka sab se aham sawaal. Ye dekhna hai ke RM khud is darmiyan kahan khari thi — kya woh bhi phansi hui thi, ya usne raise karna chhor diya tha.
Follow-up: "Kabhi koi cheez upar raise ki? Us ka kya bana?"
6Naye tools ka teachers aur aap ke coaches par kya asar dekha aap ne?
Khula rakhiye. "Asaan hua ya mushkil?" — dono option dena zaroori hai, warna leading ho jata hai.
7RM level par aapas ki communication — kis baat ki line hai aur kis ki nahi — us mein koi cheez behtar honi chahiye?
Bilkul neutral rakhiye, ilzaam ki shakal na de. Agar woh khud maan le ke "haan, hum log aksar coaches ki baaten kar lete hain," to ye finding bohat qeemti hai. Agar woh khud na uthaye, zabardasti mat khodiye.
8Aap ki jagah jo naya RM aayega — usay pehle din se kya pata hona chahiye?
Log apne liye shikayat nahi karte, "naye bande" ke liye sach bol dete hain. Aksar poore interview ka sab se seedha jawab yahan aata hai.

5Khatma

Koi aisi baat jo aap chahti hain main leadership tak pohanchaon?
Kuch aur jo main ne nahi poocha aur aap batana chahti hain?
Closing — ye keh kar khatam kijiye

"Sara, bohat shukriya — ye sab bohat kaam ki baatein hain. Main ye likh kar aage bhejunga.

Aap ne jo waqt aur mehnat yahan di, us ka idare ko faida hua hai. Allah aap ko aagay kamyab kare."

6Do usool — ye bhoolna nahi

1. Defend mat kijiye

Jab woh kisi cheez ki shikayat kare, "nahi aisa nahi hai" ya "asal mein wajah ye thi" mat kahiye. Bas "aur batayein" kahiye. Jis lamhe aap defend karenge, us lamhe woh sach bolna band kar degi.

2. Khamoshi bhardijiye mat

Sawaal ke baad agar woh chup ho jaye, aap bhi chup rahiye. 3–4 second ki khamoshi ke baad log wahi baat bolte hain jo pehle rok rahe thay. Is poori sheet ki sab se kaargar cheez yahi hai.
