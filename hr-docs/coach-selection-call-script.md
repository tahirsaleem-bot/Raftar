# Coach Selection Call Script — Taleemabad / NIETE
**Caller:** Jawwad Ali (HR) | **Project timeline:** till December 2026

---

## Call se pehle (30 seconds)
- [ ] Candidate ka naam, district, salary figure saamne rakho
- [ ] Joining date aur reporting manager confirm
- [ ] Notepad ready — 4 cheezein note karni hain (neeche list)

## SCRIPT

**[1] OPENING**

"Assalam-o-Alaikum. Am I speaking with **[Candidate name]**?

Ji, this is **Jawwad Ali** from the **HR department at Taleemabad — NIETE**. I hope I'm not catching you at a bad time — kya aap ke paas 5–7 minutes hain?"

> *Busy ho to:* "No problem at all. Main aap ko **[time]** pe call kar leta hoon — would that work for you?"

---

**[2] THE GOOD NEWS**

"So first of all — **congratulations!** Aap ka interview process complete ho gaya hai, and I'm pleased to inform you ke we have **selected** you for the position of **Coach** at Taleemabad.

The panel was quite impressed with your profile and your responses during the interview — isi liye aap ko finalize kiya gaya."

> *Pause — reaction sunno.*

---

**[3] ROLE & TERMS**

"Let me quickly walk you through the details:

- **Position:** Coach — [Program name]
- **Station / District:** [_____]
- **Reporting to:** [Manager name]
- **Joining date:** [_____]
- **Monthly compensation:** PKR [_____], plus [allowance]
- **Working days & timings:** [_____]
- **Engagement type:** Project-based contract

Ye sab formally aap ke **offer letter** mein documented hoga."

> *Numbers slow bolo, aur candidate se repeat karwao.*

---

**[4] PROJECT TIMELINE — transparency (candidate ke poochhne se PEHLE bolo)**

"Ek baat pe main **completely transparent** rehna chahta hoon — ye ek **project-based engagement** hai, and the current project cycle runs **till December 2026**.

Practically iska matlab: aap ka contract **December tak** ka hoga. **Extension** do cheezon pe depend karta hai — **project ka next phase / funding**, aur aap ki **individual performance**. Agar project extend hota hai aur performance strong rehti hai, to absolutely, we would want to continue with you.

But main aap ko koi **false guarantee** nahi dena chahta — I'd rather you make this decision with complete clarity."

---

**[5] NATURE OF THE ROLE**

"One more important thing — ye ek **field-based role** hai, it's not a desk job. Aap ko assigned schools ki **regular visits** karni hongi, teachers ke saath **classroom observations**, **feedback sessions** aur **on-site coaching**, along with **monthly reporting and data entry**.

So there's a fair amount of travel involved. I just want to confirm — is that comfortable for you?"

---

**[6] CONFIRMATION**

"Great. So can I take this as a **confirmation** that you're accepting the offer, and that you'll be available to join by **[date]**?"

> *Notice period issue:* "That's completely understandable. Aap realistically bata dein kab tak join kar sakte hain — main internally discuss kar ke confirm kar dunga."

---

**[7] DOCUMENTS**

"For onboarding, humein kuch documents chahiye hongi. Main WhatsApp pe complete list bhej deta hoon, but broadly:

1. CNIC — front and back
2. Educational degrees and certificates
3. Experience letters, if any
4. Recent passport-size photograph
5. Bank details — account title, number, IBAN, branch
6. Two professional references — name, designation, contact number

Agar aap **[deadline]** tak share kar dein, we can process your joining on time."

---

**[8] NEXT STEPS**

"Here's how it moves forward:

- Aaj/kal aap ko **offer letter** email pe milega — review kar ke, sign kar ke wapis share kar dein
- Documents verify hone ke baad **joining confirmation** issue hoga
- **[Date]** ko **orientation and coach training** hai — details separately share karunga

Can you please confirm your **email address** for me?"

> *Email repeat karke verify karo.*

---

**[9] CLOSING**

"Perfect. Koi **question** ho, please feel free to ask… *(pause)*

Alright then — **welcome aboard!** Main aap ko offer letter bhej raha hoon jisme project timeline aur sab terms clearly mentioned hain. Kisi bhi confusion ke liye isi number pe WhatsApp kar dein.

**Congratulations once again.** Allah Hafiz."

---

## OBJECTION HANDLING

**"Sirf December tak? Ye bohat short hai."**
"It's a fair concern. Do baatein — **one**, extension ka genuine possibility hai, decision project ke next phase ke saath aayega. **Two**, ye mahine aap ke liye **Taleemabad ka experience** aur strong **field coaching portfolio** ban jayenge, jo development sector mein value rakhta hai — aur hamari internal openings mein aap ki priority hogi."

**"December ke baad permanent ho jaunga?"**
"Main honestly bataunga — abhi main aisa koi commitment nahi kar sakta, kyunke ye project continuation pe depend karta hai. Jo main **commit** kar sakta hoon: extension ya koi doosri opening aane pe aap **already-tested internal candidate** honge."

**"Salary barha sakte hain?"**
"Main aap ki baat note kar leta hoon aur **HR head se discuss kar ke kal tak revert** karta hoon."
> *Call pe kabhi commit na karo.*

**"Current job chhorni paray gi is duration ke liye?"**
"That's a genuinely important decision, main aap ko rush nahi karna chahta. Aap ko **[24–48 hours]** de deta hoon — ghar mein discuss kar ke bataiye. Offer letter bhej deta hoon taake terms written form mein saamne hon."

**"Saath doosri job dekh sakta hoon?"**
"Aap ka career planning aap ka haq hai. Meri request sirf ye hai ke contract duration mein **full commitment** ho, aur koi transition ho to **proper notice** ke saath, taake schools aur teachers affected na hon."

## TONE RULES
- **Terminology English mein** — position, compensation, onboarding, orientation, contract, references → professional signal
- **Reassurance Urdu mein** — "koi masla nahi", "bilkul samajh sakta hoon", "aap fikar na karein" → comfort
- Har section ke baad **chhota confirmation sawal** — "Is that clear?" / "Aap ke liye workable hai?"
- Salary aur dates **do baar** bolo
- Negotiation pe **kabhi on-call commit nahi**
